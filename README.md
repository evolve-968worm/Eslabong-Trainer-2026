## 🎮 About the Game

Eslabong is a medieval arena combat and mercenary club management game developed and published by shirowita. It entered Early Access on August 24, 2026, for Windows PC. Players control fighters in real-time battles, manage a roster, recruit mercenaries, and compete across leagues, cups, and tournaments.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Eslabong**. This trainer enhances Eslabong's arena battles and club-management systems with options for fighter survivability, gold, roster development, recruitment, and seasonal progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with team compositions, reduce the grind of recruiting and upgrading mercenaries, practice arena encounters, or explore league and tournament progression with fewer resource limitations.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

In direct-control battles, move your selected fighter around the arena and use positioning to avoid attacks and create openings. The trainer does not replace movement or aiming, so activate combat options before engaging opponents and keep normal control over navigation.

### Tools & Inventory

Eslabong focuses on building, equipping, and managing a mercenary roster rather than using a traditional item-heavy inventory. Gold-related options are most useful in the market and club-management screens, where you recruit mercenaries and improve your team.

### World & Environment

The campaign advances through seasons of structured leagues, cups, and tournaments. Rosters, opponents, and statistics can change between runs. Use progression options after a match has fully ended and the results have been recorded.

### Combat & Weapons

Arena battles can be played by directly controlling a fighter in real time or by setting tactics and allowing the team to fight automatically. Enable health or auto-battle options before the match begins, then use the normal controls to aim abilities, position fighters, and respond to enemy actions.
